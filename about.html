Who we are

With six decades of impact, PUD stands as a multinational leader in urban planning, architecture and sustainable tourism — offering strategic consultancy, design excellence and delivery management, from visionary master planning to precise execution.

Operating across Cairo, Dubai and Riyadh, PUD blends deep regional insight with global expertise to deliver innovative, culturally grounded, tailored and sustainable projects.

Our strength lies in a holistic, end-to-end approach, powered by a network of multidisciplinary experts spanning consultancy, design, architecture, planning and project management.

Guided by the values of leadership, harmony and clarity, we go beyond traditional consultancy by transferring knowledge, building capabilities and empowering our partners to achieve enduring, future-ready outcomes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is PUD Consultants?

PUD (Planning Urban Development) Consultants is a multidisciplinary planning and design consultancy founded in Cairo in 1966 by Prof. Abdallah Abdel Aziz Attia. It shapes sustainable cities, tourism destinations, heritage areas and sacred landscapes across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and beyond.

Who leads PUD Consultants today?

PUD is led by Prof. Dr. Amr A. Attia, CEO and Principal, Professor of Architecture and Urban Planning at Ain Shams University, an Eisenhower Fellow (2007) and former Vice President of Strategic Development at WATG and Principal Director at AECOM.

What services does PUD offer?

Urban and regional planning, master planning and urban design, architectural design, sustainable tourism development, cultural heritage, landscape planning and design, environment and sustainability, green economy, development consultancy, and financial planning and assessment.

Where does PUD work?

PUD has delivered 150+ projects in 11 countries and 60+ cities, with offices in Cairo (Egypt), Riyadh (Saudi Arabia) and Dubai (UAE). Work spans Egypt from Alexandria to Aswan, Saudi Arabia from NEOM to Jazan, and countries including Kuwait, Iraq, Syria, Palestine, Iran, Cameroon, Gambia and Germany.

What are some of PUD’s notable projects?

Highlights include the Pyramids Plateau Destination and AKHET tourism master plan (with WATG), Al Mashaaer Al Mugaddassah in Makkah, Alamein Riviera, Sumou Boulevard, Cairo Media City, strategic advisory for the New Administrative Capital, the Khaybar Heritage Area and AlUla County framework plans, and Jazan Economic City.

How does PUD approach sustainability?

Sustainability is at the core of every PUD project, through environmental responsibility, social inclusion, cultural heritage preservation and green building standards. PUD’s work aligns with UN Sustainable Development Goals 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13 and 17.

Does PUD work with international firms?

Yes. PUD regularly collaborates with leading global firms including WATG, AECOM, SOM, BuroHappold, HOK, HILL International and Giugiaro, combining international expertise with deep local knowledge, alongside 100+ clients and 90+ collaborations over six decades.
